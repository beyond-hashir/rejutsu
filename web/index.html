MESH STUDIO / A clearer picture of motion.Built for your local workflow
THREE SIMPLE STEPS

From footage to facial motion.

  1. Add your pair.

    Select the original color video and its matching depth-map export. Keep the same framing and start point.

  2. Create your wireframe.

    The local engine checks timing, tracks faces, and draws the mesh onto the depth clip. You can keep using this page while it works.

  3. Preview and save.

    Review the final clip or the side-by-side comparison. Save video opens a location picker when your browser supports it, or downloads the file.

Turned or obscured faces may have gaps in the mesh. This app assumes matching framing; use the comparison to check alignment. Runs stay in the project’s outputs/web folder. All processing happens on this PC.
